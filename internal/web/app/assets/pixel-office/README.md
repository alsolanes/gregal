# Pixel Office assets

The character crops, `furniture.png`, and `LICENSE.txt` come from the Pixel Office Asset Pack by 2dPig:
https://2dpig.itch.io/pixel-office

The artist releases the pack under CC0 1.0. The included `LICENSE.txt` is the license text provided with the download. The files are bundled here so the office works offline.

Characters are cropped from the original sheet. Flag decorations are removed from the furniture sheet. The original precomposed scene is not bundled; the application arranges the furniture and four moving agents itself.

`newspaper.png` is the Newspaper icon by Delapouite, from
https://game-icons.net/1x1/delapouite/newspaper.html,
licensed separately under CC BY 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . The bundled image is unmodified;
the scene scales and rotates it when displayed. This attribution must be retained.
